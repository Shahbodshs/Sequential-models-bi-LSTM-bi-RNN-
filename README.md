# RNN, LSTM, and Bidirectional Networks

Lecture notes covering the foundations of recurrent neural networks, with an emphasis on the mathematics of forward propagation and backpropagation through time (BPTT).

## Contents

- **RNNs:** Hidden-state updates, next-token prediction, softmax, cross-entropy loss, and BPTT.
- **Gradient flow:** Recurrent Jacobians, including why the derivative takes the form $D_tW_h$, and the vanishing/exploding gradient problems.
- **LSTMs:** Cell states, forget/input/output gates, candidate memory, and full forward and backward equations.
- **Bidirectional models:** BiRNN and BiLSTM architectures, their forward/backward passes, parameter gradients, and shared-input gradients.
- **Reference:** Collected equations and comparisons of the architectures.
